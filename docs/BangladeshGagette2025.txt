**No. 40.00.0000.016.99.009.24-207** — In light of the recommendations made by the "Minimum Wage Re-evaluation and Annual Increment" committee, formed under the Ministry of Labour and Employment's Memo No. 40.00.0000.042.41.004.24-241, dated 29-09-2024, the Government has announced the annual increment for the garment industry sector as follows:

1. It is recommended to increase the annual increment to a total of 9% by adding an additional 4% to the existing 5% wage increase;


2. The annual increment determined by the Minimum Wage Board, i.e., 5%, shall continue to remain in effect;


3. The 4% annual increment increase recommended by the committee will be effective from December 1, 2024, which must be paid along with the salary payable in the month of January 2025;


4. Along with the wage increase, other benefits related to the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (latest amendment) shall also be applicable;


5. This wage increase will remain in effect until re-evaluated by the Government or until the next minimum wage rate is announced by the Minimum Wage Board.
